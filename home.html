How it works
01
⬡
Decompose
Your question is broken into distinct research facets — each one a targeted search that covers a different angle of the answer.
—
02
◎
Search & score
Free sources are searched and scored for relevance. Hits that genuinely answer the sub-query are marked covered. Weak coverage is flagged as a gap.
—
03
◈
Buy only gaps
For each gap, paid sources are priced and ranked. You choose what to fetch — no flat subscription, no overpaying for content you don't need.
